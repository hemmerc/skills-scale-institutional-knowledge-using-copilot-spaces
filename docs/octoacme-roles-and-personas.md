# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Specialists

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Specialists ensure that features meet quality standards before release. They work across the delivery lifecycle to validate acceptance criteria, uncover defects early, and support confident product decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain test plans aligned to acceptance criteria
- Execute unit, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Validate features against defined quality gates and release readiness checks
- Document defects with clear reproduction steps and expected outcomes
- Partner with developers to improve testability and reduce regression risk
- Support smoke testing and post-deployment verification
- Share quality trends, risk areas, and improvement opportunities with the team

### Goals
- Reduce production defects and user-facing issues
- Improve quality coverage across critical workflows
- Help the team release with confidence
- Ensure acceptance criteria are objectively and consistently validated

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- Acceptance criteria reviews
- Defect triage and QA reports
- Release readiness checklists and sign-off discussions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate changes and identify regressions
- Aligns with Product Managers and Project Managers to confirm requirements are testable and complete
- Provides critical inputs to release decisions and escalation discussions

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Lead provides strategic oversight for product direction, prioritization, and cross-project alignment. They help resolve competing priorities, guide product decisions, and support Product Managers in executing outcomes effectively.

### Responsibilities
- Provide strategic direction and guardrails for product initiatives
- Align roadmap priorities across projects and stakeholders
- Resolve escalations related to scope, sequencing, and trade-offs
- Support Product Managers in decision-making and stakeholder communication
- Review major plans, milestones, and high-impact changes
- Ensure business value and customer impact remain central to delivery decisions

### Goals
- Maintain strategic alignment across initiatives
- Improve prioritization quality and delivery focus
- Reduce ambiguity and decision latency
- Support sustainable product execution at scale

### Typical Communication
- Weekly leadership and PM syncs
- Roadmap and portfolio reviews
- Escalation conversations for blockers or competing priorities
- Sponsor and stakeholder updates

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Product Managers to refine strategy and priorities
- Collaborates with Project Managers to align delivery timing and constraints
- Escalates business or cross-team trade-offs that require sponsorship or leadership intervention
- Supports Developers and QA by clarifying outcome intent and trade-offs

---

## Project Sponsor / Business Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors represent the business interests of the initiative and provide sponsorship, approval authority, and strategic oversight. They ensure that the project remains aligned with organizational goals and stakeholder expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals, priorities, and strategic value
- Approve scope, funding, and major trade-off decisions
- Sponsor cross-functional alignment and support when issues arise
- Monitor outcomes and expected business impact
- Support decision-making when external dependencies or organizational priorities change

### Goals
- Ensure the initiative delivers measurable business value
- Maintain alignment with strategic priorities
- Support timely decisions and organizational sponsorship

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee updates
- Milestone reviews and approval checkpoints
- Business impact and roadmap discussions
- Escalation of major risks or blockers

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Product Leads and Product Managers to confirm business intent
- Provides leadership and decision support to Project Managers
- Reviews progress against expected value and strategic outcomes
- May influence scope and prioritization decisions through sponsorship authority

---

## Scrum Master / Iteration Lead

### Role Summary
Scrum Master or Iteration Lead facilitates effective team delivery by improving workflow, removing blockers, and helping the team operate consistently within agreed processes.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help the team identify and remove blockers
- Support continuous improvement and team process health
- Track sprint or iteration flow and promote sustainable delivery
- Foster clear communication and accountability across the team
- Support healthy working agreements and escalation when needed

### Goals
- Improve team effectiveness and delivery consistency
- Reduce blockers and unproductive work
- Increase transparency and team alignment
- Support continuous improvement without adding bureaucracy

### Typical Communication
- Daily or iteration-based team syncs
- Retrospectives and backlog refinement sessions
- Team health check-ins and workflow reviews
- Escalation support for process or dependency concerns

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Developers and QA to improve flow and reduce friction
- Partners with Project Managers on scheduling, dependencies, and facilitation
- Supports Product Managers and Product Leads by keeping delivery rhythm healthy and predictable

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that project work meets required safety, privacy, and governance standards. They help the team identify and mitigate risk before deployment and during incident response.

### Responsibilities
- Review designs, workflows, and technical decisions for security and compliance impact
- Define or validate security requirements and controls
- Participate in risk reviews and architectural discussions
- Ensure compliance with applicable policies, standards, and regulations
- Support incident response planning and security review workflows
- Help the team remediate high-risk issues before release

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Protect customer and organizational data
- Improve secure-by-default practices
- Maintain trust in release quality and operational readiness

### Typical Communication
- Design and risk review meetings
- Security and compliance checkpoints
- Incident communications and remediation briefings
- Release governance discussions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Developers and Technical Architects to validate controls and design choices
- Supports Project Managers and Product Leads on risk communication and escalation
- Collaborates with QA on release readiness when security and compliance checks are required

---

## Technical Architect / Lead Developer

### Role Summary
Technical Architect or Lead Developer helps shape the technical direction of the project. They ensure the solution is robust, scalable, and aligned with team capabilities and system constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and architecture decisions
- Review implementation approaches for scalability, maintainability, and integration complexity
- Identify dependencies, technical risks, and architectural trade-offs
- Support developer teams with patterns, standards, and technical problem solving
- Ensure technical decisions align with system constraints and delivery goals

### Goals
- Maintain sound technical direction and design quality
- Reduce avoidable rework and architectural drift
- Support maintainable and scalable implementation
- Enable teams to make informed technical trade-offs

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical planning and dependency mapping
- Cross-team technical alignment sessions
- Risk and mitigation reviews

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Developers to reinforce standards and technical decisions
- Supports Product Managers and Product Leads on feasibility, sequencing, and trade-offs
- Collaborates with QA to improve testability and release confidence
- Advises Project Managers on technical dependencies and delivery risk
